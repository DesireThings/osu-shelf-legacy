This is a minor release. The desktop app can now find collections on osu!Collector for you instead of needing a pasted link, the collections list can be filtered and sorted, and Settings shows each mirror's known limits. It's in early access: versions stay at 0.x until it's stable enough for 1.0.0.

osu-shelf is an unofficial community tool, not made, endorsed or supported by ppy (osu!) or osu!Collector.

## What's new since 0.4.0

- **Browse collections.** A new screen in the desktop app searches osu!Collector by name, uploader or words from the description, and opens on the month's popular collections. Sort by newest, most favourites or most difficulties, or by popularity over a week, month, year or all time when the search box is empty. Narrow the results by minimum and maximum difficulties, stars and main mode, then **Add** a collection to your list or **Open on osu!Collector** to see its page in your browser. **Review your list** takes you on to step 2 to download as usual.
- **Filter and sort your collections.** Once you've added two or more collections, you can filter them by name or uploader and sort them newest first, oldest first, by name or by most maps. Lookups and errors stay at the top while you filter, and **Download all** still covers the whole list.
- **Mirror limits in Settings.** Each mirror in the Mirrors card now shows its known rate-limit and download budget after the host, or "limits unknown" when it publishes none. This is display only: downloads still follow the live headers the mirrors send, so a stale summary can't affect them.
- **Plainer errors when adding a collection.** A failed Add, whether from a pasted link or from Browse, now says what went wrong and what to try (the collection doesn't exist, osu!Collector is limiting requests, or it couldn't be reached) instead of showing an HTTP status or network detail.

## Known limits

- **Browse can only filter what it has loaded.** osu!Collector's listing has no tags and no size, star or mode filters, so those apply to the rows already fetched, and **Load more** fetches up to three more pages to find matches. Star ranges are approximate, and sizes count difficulties, not map sets.
- **Listing counts can be out of date.** A collection's difficulty count in the listing can differ from what you get when you add it (for example 354 against 422).
- **Changing the sort needs Search pressed** before it takes effect.
- **The Windows and macOS builds haven't been run on a real Windows PC or Mac yet.** That covers the installers, the Windows one-line installer, and finding osu!lazer and Torii folders on those systems. They're built and checked automatically; please [open an issue](https://github.com/DesireThings/osu-shelf/issues/new) if something doesn't work.
- **macOS:** Apple Silicon only; there are no Intel Mac builds.
- **The desktop app doesn't update itself.** To update, download and install the new version over the old one. The terminal app does update itself.
- **Find in the desktop app** lists map sets only: there's no per-difficulty view yet, and searches through osu! don't show download sizes.
- **Update in the desktop app** lists missing map sets by number (each links to its osu! page), not by title, and can't yet mark a set as already installed or check failed maps again.

## Checking the files

`SHA256SUMS` lists the SHA-256 of every file in this release. On Linux or macOS, in the folder with your downloads: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing` (on macOS, `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`).
